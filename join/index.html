Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible to apply for Andropedia?

Any undergraduate or postgraduate student with an active appetite for engineering, design, or community building. We welcome 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year students across all branches.

How does the weekly task and evaluation cycle work?

Once inducted, each domain assigns weekly challenges tailored to current industry and research demands. Members submit deliverables via our portal, which domain leads evaluate on technical depth, innovation, completion, and documentation. Scores update the live leaderboard.

What is the expected weekly time commitment?

Typically 6 to 10 hours per week, covering the weekly task sprint, domain sync discussions, and collaborative weekend hack sessions.

Can I contribute to more than one domain?

Yes! While each member has a primary domain for weekly evaluations and leaderboard tracking, cross-domain collaboration on hackathons and flagship projects is strongly encouraged.

Can I apply more than once?

Only one application is accepted per email address, so take your time to make it count. If you made a mistake, contact the club using the email you receive after applying.
